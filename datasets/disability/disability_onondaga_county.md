# Lender Center — Disability: Onondaga County

**Source:** U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (ACS) 5-Year Estimates, compiled by the Lender Center for Social Justice as the “Disability Database”
**Data last updated:** March 2026
**Geography:** Onondaga County
**Years covered:** 2013–2023 (ACS 5-year estimates; each year is a 5-year average ending in that year, not a single-year value)

**Read this before using these figures.** Every count in this document is a count of people **with a disability** — this database contains no total-population denominator, so prevalence rates ("what share of residents have a disability") cannot be computed from it alone. A total population figure for Onondaga County IS available in the connected data — the Demographic & Housing Estimates document for Onondaga County (ACS 5-Year 2019–2023). Cite that document explicitly for the denominator and note that the two documents have different "Data last updated" values. One caveat if you do compute a share: the counts here are for the civilian noninstitutionalized population, while the Demographic & Housing Estimates total is the whole resident population. The two universes are close but not identical, so describe any resulting share as approximate and say which denominator you used. Apart from any route explicitly described just above, do not substitute a total from a different geography, a different year, or a different universe to fill the gap — say the denominator is unavailable instead. The source spreadsheet labels the first column "Total civilian noninstitutionalized population"; that label is incorrect — the column is the population with a disability (its male/female counts and its race counts each sum to it exactly), and it has been renamed here accordingly.

Each row is one indicator; each column is one ACS 5-year vintage, newest first. `—` means the source reported no value for that indicator and year.
