# Lender Center — Disability: United States

**Source:** U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (ACS) 5-Year Estimates, compiled by the Lender Center for Social Justice as the “Disability Database”
**Data last updated:** March 2026
**Geography:** United States
**Years covered:** 2013–2023 (ACS 5-year estimates; each year is a 5-year average ending in that year, not a single-year value)

**Read this before using these figures.** Every count in this document is a count of people **with a disability** — this database contains no total-population denominator, so prevalence rates ("what share of residents have a disability") cannot be computed from it alone. There is **no** total population figure for United States anywhere in the connected data, and none can be derived from it — the Demographic & Housing Estimates documents cover Cayuga, Cortland, Madison, Onondaga and Oswego counties only, and the education documents give population 18 and over but not under-18. For United States, prevalence rates cannot be produced at all; say so plainly and offer the counts instead. Apart from any route explicitly described just above, do not substitute a total from a different geography, a different year, or a different universe to fill the gap — say the denominator is unavailable instead. The source spreadsheet labels the first column "Total civilian noninstitutionalized population"; that label is incorrect — the column is the population with a disability (its male/female counts and its race counts each sum to it exactly), and it has been renamed here accordingly.

Each row is one indicator; each column is one ACS 5-year vintage, newest first. `—` means the source reported no value for that indicator and year.
