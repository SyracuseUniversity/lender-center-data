# Lender Center — Educational Attainment: Central New York

**Source:** U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (ACS) 5-Year Estimates, compiled by the Lender Center for Social Justice as the “Education Database”
**Data last updated:** April 2026
**Geography:** Central New York
**Years covered:** 2014–2024 (ACS 5-year estimates; each year is a 5-year average ending in that year, not a single-year value)

Counts labelled "Population 25 years and over: <group>" are the 25-and-over universe for that group and are the denominator for the attainment rows beneath them; the race groups sum exactly to "Population 25 years and over". Hispanic or Latino origin overlaps the race categories and is not additive with them. Median earnings are dollars, not counts.

Each row is one indicator; each column is one ACS 5-year vintage, newest first. `—` means the source reported no value for that indicator and year.
