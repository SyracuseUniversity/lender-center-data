# Lender Center — Cross-Geography Comparison: Housing

**Source:** U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (ACS) 5-Year Estimates, compiled by the Lender Center for Social Justice as the “Housing Database”
**Data last updated:** March 2026
**Geographies compared:** City of Syracuse, Onondaga County, Central New York, New York State, United States
**Year shown:** 2023 ACS 5-year estimates (a 5-year average ending 2023). Earlier years back to 2013 are in the single-geography documents.

This document exists so that questions comparing geographies can be answered from one place. The single-geography documents hold every year and every indicator; this holds the headline figures side by side.

**Central New York has no usable median here.** Central New York is not a Census geography but a five-county region the Lender Center defines, so its figures are constructed from the five counties. That works for counts and not for medians, so every median below is withheld for Central New York. The single-geography documents explain this in full. When ranking geographies by a median, rank the four that have one and say Central New York cannot be ranked.
