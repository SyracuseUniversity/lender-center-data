**Source:** U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (ACS) 5-Year Estimates, compiled by the Lender Center for Social Justice as the “Education Database”
**Data last updated:** April 2026
**Geographies compared:** City of Syracuse, Onondaga County, Central New York, New York State, United States
**Year shown:** 2024 ACS 5-year estimates (a 5-year average ending 2024). Earlier years back to 2014 are in the single-geography documents.

This document exists so that questions comparing geographies can be answered from one place. The single-geography documents hold every year and every indicator; this holds the headline figures side by side.
