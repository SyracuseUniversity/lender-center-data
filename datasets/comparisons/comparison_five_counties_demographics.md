# Lender Center — Cross-County Comparison: Demographic & Housing Estimates

**Source:** U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (ACS) 5-Year Estimates, table DP05 (ACS Demographic and Housing Estimates)
**Data last updated:** ACS 5-Year Estimates 2019–2023
**Geographies compared:** Cayuga County, Cortland County, Madison County, Onondaga County, Oswego County — the five Central New York counties
**Provided by:** Lender Center for Social Justice data export `ACSDP5Y2023.DP05` (retrieved 2026-07-27)

This document exists so that questions comparing counties, or asking about Central New York as a whole, can be answered from one place. The single-county documents hold the full detail; this holds the headline figures side by side.

**Central New York is exactly these five counties.** Verified against the Lender Center's own regional totals: the education database's 2023 population 18-and-over for Central New York is 619,011, and these five counties' 18-and-over figures sum to 619,011 — an exact match. Rows labelled "Central New York (five-county total)" are therefore sums of the five county figures above them, not separately published values.
